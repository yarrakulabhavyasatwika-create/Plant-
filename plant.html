Plant Care

Simple Tips for Happy Plants

Great plants start with great care. Follow these basics and watch your greenery thrive.

☀️

Right Light

Most houseplants thrive in bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid harsh direct rays that can scorch leaves.

💧

Water Wisely

Check soil moisture before watering. Overwatering is the #1 cause of plant problems — when in doubt, don't.

🌡️

Temperature & Humidity

Most tropical plants love warmth and humidity. Keep away from AC vents and cold drafts.

🪴

Repot When Ready

When roots start peeking out the bottom, it's time for a bigger home. Spring is ideal repotting season.
